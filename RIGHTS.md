# Rights and contributions

This is an unofficial, noncommercial fan and educational English translation project for *Gintama: Gintoki vs. Hijikata!? Kabukichou Gintama Dai Soudatsusen!!* on Nintendo DS. It does not translate the manga, anime or the whole Gintama franchise.

I do not own Gintama, the original game or its characters, story, artwork, music, original text or trademarks. Those rights remain with their respective holders. No affiliation, endorsement or rights-holder permission is implied.

## Piracy and rights-holder requests

I do not support piracy and discourage it. Use only your own legally obtained ROM. Do not share game files or link to unauthorized downloads.

I take rights concerns seriously. If Bandai Namco, Hideaki Sorachi or another rights holder or authorized representative contacts me, I will promptly review the request and verify the sender's authority and the material concerned. For a verified request, I will remove or disable the affected material I control and stop the related work. If the concern covers the project as a whole, this may mean taking it offline. I will follow GitHub's takedown process and applicable legal requirements.

This covers public repositories, releases, website files and other published project copies I control. I cannot guarantee removal of independent forks, clones, caches or third-party archives.

## What the license covers

The project's code, original documentation and original pixel alphabet use [Apache 2.0](LICENSE). This license covers that project material only. The PSF notice and license for the HTML entity lookup appear below.

The license does not cover the game, its artwork or its story. This is an unofficial translation; permission from the game's rights holders has not been confirmed.

## Local game files

Use only your own legally obtained, matching ROM. Keep it, extracted assets, source text, saves, game screenshots and rebuilt copies on your computer. Do not upload them here or link to unauthorized downloads. The browser reads selected game files locally; it does not upload them. Hash checks identify the supported game revision, not ownership.

Public packages include tools, templates, English translations and UI labels, functional metadata, original lettering and three editor tutorial images. The tutorial images show the editor without loaded game artwork or dialogue.

The English dialogue in `translations/ready/` and `reference/`, including the opening and battle translations, adapts the game's narrative. It is not just indexing data. Translated UI labels and other replacement text may also involve rights in the underlying game. Under U.S. law, translations can be derivative works; see the [Copyright Office's explanation](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf). Permission for this adaptation has not been verified. Keeping the Japanese script and ROM out of the repository does not resolve that question. Fair use depends on the circumstances, with [no fixed safe word count or percentage](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html). Using JSON, a script or a patch file does not itself establish permission.

Source-asset hashes and file-boundary checks help detect accidental data uploads. They do not determine whether translated dialogue, labels, recipes or screenshots may lawfully be distributed. The maintainer has chosen to publish the playable fan translation with these limits stated, rather than describe it as a tools-only package.

## Contributions and publication

Only submit material you have the rights to share. Code and original documentation contributions use Apache 2.0. Translation suggestions permit review and reuse of your own wording to the extent you control it; this does not license the underlying game. Do not copy official translations or another fan project's writing without permission.

The file audit catches common accidental game-data and secret uploads. It cannot establish legal clearance. Review the actual material before publication, including patch payloads: a delta can contain protected bytes. Website deployment and public exports require explicit maintainer configuration. See [publishing](https://github.com/zthegin/Gintama---Gintoki-vs-Hijikata-game-english-translation/blob/main/docs/PUBLISHING.md).

For a rights concern, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile and identify the affected files. Keep personal information, legal correspondence and game files out of public issues. Formal notices follow [GitHub's DMCA procedure](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).
