    Try your battle edit
    
        
    Download the battle JSON. Put it beside the project files, then build a new local ROM from your original legally obtained ROM:
    
        
    python tools/build_ui.py --rom "original.nds" --battle "battle_translations.json" --out "local/battle-test.nds"
    
        
    This overlays your battle edits on the bundled battle, opening, field and graphics translations. Your original ROM is left unchanged. Add --dialogue "your-batch.json" if you also want to test a downloaded field batch. Shorten any message the builder rejects, then download it again.
